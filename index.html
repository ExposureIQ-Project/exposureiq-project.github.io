01 / CAPABILITIES

Your attack surface doesn't stand still.

An overlooked subdomain or unexpected service is easier to investigate when you can see it alongside your other assets and previous scan results.

01 — DISCOVER

See the outside view

Find public-facing subdomains, addresses, and services within your authorized scope.

02 — INSPECT

Follow the finding

Navigate scan results and review the context behind flagged exposures in the TUI.

03 — COMPARE

Know what changed

Use saved scans and the diff view to focus on changes instead of treating every scan as a fresh list.

✳

Coming later: optional AI assistance. The planned helper is intended to work from scan results, not conduct scans or make decisions on its own. The core ExposureIQ workflow does not depend on it.

02 / WORKFLOW

From scope to signal.

A terminal-first path from the assets you are authorized to test to the changes worth investigating.

  1. [ 01 ]

    Define scope

    Start with a domain you own or have permission to test.

  2. [ 02 ]

    Discover

    Review identified subdomains, hosts, services, and ports.

  3. [ 03 ]

    Investigate

    Inspect findings and navigate their supporting details.

  4. [ 04 ]

    Compare

    Check saved results to understand what's changed.
